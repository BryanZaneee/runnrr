# BZS Software — overview

BZS Software helps service-based businesses turn messy customer, document, and internal workflows into clean, AI-assisted systems. We map how leads, jobs, messages, files, and follow-ups move through a business, then build practical tools that help teams respond faster, recover missed revenue, cut repetitive admin work, and make better decisions, while keeping humans in control.

## Who it is for

Service businesses with repeatable customer, document, scheduling, and follow-up workflows. The initial focus is home services: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, landscaping, pressure washing, pool service, and cleaning. The same approach also fits clinics, dental and med-spa offices, law firms, real estate teams, insurance agencies, gyms, accounting firms, and B2B agencies.

## How we work

We map the path work takes, then build AI that handles the drafting and chasing while a person approves anything that reaches a customer. AI drafts the busywork; your team approves every message before it goes out. Nothing sends on its own unless you decide to trust a workflow to auto-send.

The engagement usually moves Map → Build → Run: a free workflow review to find the highest-leverage fixes, a scoped build around your existing tools, and optional managed tuning after launch.

## Principles

- Human-in-the-loop: a person stays on the decisions that matter.
- No rip-and-replace: we build around the CRM, calendar, messaging, and invoicing tools you already use.
- Measurable outcomes: faster first reply, fewer missed leads, automated follow-ups, more review requests.
- Your data stays yours, with scoped access and server-side credentials.
